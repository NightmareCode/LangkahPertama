About

The builder behind Langkah Pertama

Hi — I'm a full-stack web developer and designer based in Malaysia, and Langkah Pertama ("the first step") is my studio. The name is a promise: whether you're a couple sending out your wedding invitations or a restaurant going digital for the first time, I build the first step of your online journey — properly.

I work across the whole stack: design, front-end, back-end and deployment. That means no hand-offs, no miscommunication between "the designer" and "the developer" — one person who cares about the result, end to end.

Every project ships hand-coded. No drag-and-drop builders, no recycled templates — just fast, clean work that's truly yours.
