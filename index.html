I'm Far — the one behind Langkah Pertama

Hi — I'm Far, a 21-year-old full-stack web developer and designer from Malaysia, and Langkah Pertama ("the first step") is my studio. The name is a promise: whether you're a couple sending out your wedding invitations or a restaurant going digital for the first time, I build the first step of your online journey — properly.

I work across the whole stack: design, front-end, back-end and deployment. That means no hand-offs, no miscommunication between "the designer" and "the developer" — one person who cares about the result, end to end.
