// Our Story

From a state parkto a shelf of their own.

Conservationist Surin Suksuwan and biodiversity consultant Dylan Jefri Ong met on the Perlis State Park project in 2000 — each quietly dreaming of a bookshop full of nature books. In 2022, with their own savings, they decided to build it together.

They named it after the Sunda Shelf — the drowned continental shelf beneath our seas, one of the richest cradles of life on Earth. Day to day the shop is kept by Jennifer Neoh Tan, while chef Sumalee Suksuwan fills it with the smell of hand-rolled curry puffs.

"We hope these books can nurture a sense of wonder."

— Surin Suksuwan · BERNAMA
2023 The doors opened 24 June — one floor up, above the On Line Pub.

// The Café · by Maleepap

Books pair well withcurry puffs.

Chef Sumalee Suksuwan hand-rolls her famous karipap pusing in-house — reviewers call them "killer curry puffs."

Karipap Pusing · 01

The Original

Butter-crust spiral, chicken, potato & egg — the one that made the name.

Karipap Pusing · 02

Siamese Rendang

Slow, rich Siamese-style chicken rendang in a crisp spiral shell.

Karipap Pusing · 03

Sardine

The old-school classic, done properly.

Alongside: nasi lemak & bihun bungkus, Thai red curry, Vietnamese coffee, Thai milk tea, pandan tea — and old-school desserts like soya longan jelly and snow fungus soup. Come for a book, stay for lunch.

// More than a bookshop

Where nature peoplegather.

Sunda Talks

Weekend book launches, workshops and talks — from Mah Meri animal folklore to orchids, butterflies and Batu Arang history.

Nature Reads on BFM 89.9

The co-founders' recurring radio segment — 13 episodes and counting of books worth reading.

Conservation on the shelves

Art prints and mugs by Choo Beng Teong, turtle-motif batik supporting the Turtle Conservation Society — and out-of-print nature references rescued back into circulation.
