// The Story

From a hawker stall in Alor Setar to a Subang legend.

It started in 1990 — a young hawker couple frying rice with a recipe passed down from an aunt. After 16 years in Alor Setar they moved to the Klang Valley in 2007, and their corner stall in SS15 became the plate every student in Subang swears by.

Their daughter Soon Te Shian learned the wok at 13, took over right after SPM, and in 2021 moved the family stall into its own shop at Subang Square — right beside the coffee-shop corner where it all began. Today the flame burns across the Klang Valley, from SS15 to Pavilion Bukit Jalil.

"My parents always hoped that I would learn to cook and carry on the family business."

— Soon Te Shian · Free Malaysia Today
