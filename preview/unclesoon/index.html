// The Story

From a hawker stallto a Subang legend.

It started in 1990 — a young hawker couple in Alor Setar, frying rice with a recipe passed down from an aunt. In 2007 they moved to the Klang Valley, and their corner stall in SS15 became the plate every student in Subang swears by. Daughter Soon Te Shian learned the wok at 13 and moved the stall into its own shop at Subang Square in 2021.

"My parents always hoped that I would learn to cook and carry on the family business."

— Soon Te Shian · Free Malaysia Today
