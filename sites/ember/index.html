Our craft

Roasted in small drums,
tasted every single day.

We roast in 12-kilo batches so we can chase the sweet spot of every origin — never a second past it. Each batch is cupped the morning after, and only the ones we'd happily drink ourselves make it to your door.

Beans land green from farms we visit, and leave our roastery within days. That's the whole point of Ember Lane: coffee at its brightest, while it's still alive.
